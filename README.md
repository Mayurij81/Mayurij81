## 🌱 A little about me

I'm Mayuri, a Computer Science undergraduate at Walchand Institute of Technology, Solapur.

I got into technology because I was fascinated by what software could do. But the more I learn, the more interested I become in understanding **why it works, how it works, and what we should use it to build.**

I'm particularly drawn to the foundations of Computer Science. Algorithms are one of my favourite subjects because I love the process of breaking down a problem, finding patterns and thinking about how to solve it efficiently.

Beyond the technical side, I care deeply about the environment and the role technology can play in protecting it.

I want to explore **Climate Tech** — building software that helps us understand environmental challenges, use resources more thoughtfully and conserve what we already have.

I don't want to build technology just for the sake of building more technology. I want to build things that matter.

## 🌍 What I care about

- **Climate Tech:** Exploring how software, AI and data can contribute to environmental conservation.
- **Computer Science Fundamentals:** Going beyond frameworks to understand algorithms, data structures, operating systems and the foundations of computing.
- **Generative AI:** Building practical AI applications, exploring LLMs, RAG systems and multimodal interactions.
- **Problem Solving:** Learning to approach complex problems with logic, curiosity and efficient algorithms.
- **Purposeful Engineering:** Creating useful, accessible and responsible technology.

## 🍃 Selected projects

A few problems I've explored through code.

### 01. Offline Multimodal RAG Chatbot

Exploring how AI can understand and retrieve information from images, PDFs and audio without depending on cloud services.

**Focus:** Local LLMs · Retrieval-Augmented Generation · Multimodal AI

### 02. Landfill Fire Prevention System

A climate-tech project exploring the prediction of subsurface fire risks across landfill grids using environmental data and machine learning.

**Focus:** Environmental Monitoring · Geospatial Data · Predictive Modelling

### 03. Did Diwali Change the Air?

An interactive data analysis comparing modelled hourly PM2.5 concentrations across five Indian cities around Diwali 2026.

**Focus:** Climate Data · Statistical Analysis · Data Visualisation
